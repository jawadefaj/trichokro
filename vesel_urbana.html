Abstract

Eleven seats, fully electric, priced for Bangladesh.

Vesel Urbana is a compact, fully electric minibus designed by TriChokro Automotives for the fixed, short-distance routes that carry most of Dhaka's daily commuters: campus loops, factory shifts, housing societies and the last kilometre to bus and metro stations. It seats 11 passengers and a driver, reaches 70 km/h, and travels up to 100 km on a single charge with a 3.5 kW electric drivetrain.

At ৳5,00,000, Urbana puts a modern, low-noise, zero-tailpipe-emission shuttle within reach of institutions, small fleet owners and first-time transport entrepreneurs who today depend on diesel and CNG human haulers. Its wide glasshouse, enclosed cabin and clear lighting signature give passengers a safer, more dignified ride, while electric running keeps the daily cost of operation low.

The project brings TriChokro's BUET-born engineering, proven on the Pathfinder three-wheeler and the Trambulance, to the next size of urban mobility.

Built for the routes that never stop

Urbana fits wherever people travel the same few kilometres many times a day.

Campus shuttles

Universities and schools running hall-to-campus loops, like our BUET to TSC shuttle service.

Factory staff transport

RMG and industrial units moving workers between shifts and nearby residential areas.

Metro and bus feeder

Last-kilometre links between neighbourhoods and MRT or bus stations.

Housing societies

Gated communities and planned areas offering residents an internal shuttle.

Hospitals and airports

Visitor and staff shuttles between parking, gates and buildings.

Resorts and tourism

Quiet, clean guest transport where diesel noise and fumes hurt the experience.

Cabin

3 + 3 + 3 + 2. Every seat counts.

Four rows fit eleven passengers in a footprint small enough for Dhaka's side roads. Large side windows and a full-height windscreen keep the cabin bright and give the driver a clear view of the street.

Design

Three bands, one face you remember

A vermilion canopy roof, a black glass band and white body pods over each wheel. Easy to spot from across the street, easy to brand for any fleet.

Panoramic glass

Wide windows and a tall windscreen for a bright cabin and better driver visibility.

Short overhangs

Wheels pushed to the corners keep the body compact for tight turns and narrow lanes.

Quiet and clean

Electric drive means no engine rumble and no exhaust at the stop or in the cabin.
