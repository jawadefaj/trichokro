TriChokro Pathfinder

TriChokro Pathfinder is a three-wheeler designed for Bangladesh, especially Dhaka. Inspired by research around BUET’s Easy Bike Project, it focuses on safer passenger travel with an improved chassis, hydraulic braking, MacPherson suspension and a retractable hood.
