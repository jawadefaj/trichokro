Trambulance Privacy Policy

Operated by TriChokro Automotives | Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Trambulance (package identifier: com.trichokro.trambulance), an emergency medical transport and ambulance booking platform provided by TriChokro Automotives ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and web portal at trichokro.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you sign up or log in (via email or Google Sign-In), we collect your name, email address, phone number, and profile role (Patient/User or Driver).
  • Location Data (Foreground & Background):
    • For Patients: We collect your precise or approximate location to set pickup points, locate nearby hospitals, and calculate ambulance routes.
    • For Drivers: We collect real-time location data to match you with nearby ride requests and show live ambulance tracking to patients during an active trip.
  • Driver Verification Documents: If you register as a driver via trichokro.com/driver, we collect vehicle registration details, ambulance category, license numbers, and contact info for administrative verification.
  • Trip & Transaction Data: Details of your bookings, pickup/drop-off addresses, fare offers/counter-offers, payment method references (e.g., bKash, Nagad, or cash), and trip ratings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To connect patients with ambulance drivers in real time and facilitate fare negotiation.
  • To calculate routes, distances, and estimated arrival times via our mapping server (map.trichokro.com).
  • To authenticate users securely using Google OAuth and send transactional emails via our SMTP service.
  • To verify driver credentials, maintain safety standards, and provide customer support.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Information is shared only as needed to fulfill your service request:

  • Between Patients & Drivers: During a booking, the patient's name, phone number, and pickup/drop-off locations are shared with the assigned driver, and the driver's name, phone, vehicle info, and live location are shared with the patient.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted infrastructure partners including Supabase (database and authentication), Google Cloud (Google Sign-In), Brevo (transactional emails), and Oracle Cloud (dedicated map and routing server).

4. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the Trambulance app, our database, and map servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We retain your account and trip history as long as your account remains active or as needed for legal and safety audits.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time. To delete your account and associated records, please email us at noreply@trichokro.com or contact our support helpline inside the app. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data practices, please contact us:

  • Website: https://trichokro.com
  • Driver & Support Portal: https://trichokro.com/driver/
  • Location: Dhaka, Bangladesh
