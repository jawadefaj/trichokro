Project Argo, Team TriChokro, Bangladesh

The ambulance that doesn’t stop at the water’s edge.

ARKGO is an amphibious medical vehicle for flood-isolated deltas. It drives at 40 km/h on land, floats and pushes through debris-laden water at 10 km/h, and carries the patient the whole way with no transfer to a boat.
