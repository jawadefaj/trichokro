TriChokro homepage — video and Facebook section update

1. Upload index.html and youtube-feed.php together to a PHP-enabled web host.
2. Keep your existing src/assets, css, js, and images folders in place.
3. Videos use embedded YouTube players within horizontally moving cards. Hover/focus or use Pause scrolling to stop movement; mobile is swipeable.
4. youtube-feed.php attempts to retrieve the channel's latest six uploads. Without PHP access, the three supplied/known fallback embeds show instead.
5. Facebook renders a portrait 500 x 850 Page Plugin. Meta controls whether the timeline is visible and how much is scrollable; its Open Facebook Page link remains available.
6. All other home sections are retained from trichokro-index-final.html. Latest expanded bilingual news pages are included.
